Staff Privacy Notice

High Tide by Pengelly's, Looe, Cornwall  ·  Effective May 2025  ·  Review May 2026

1. Who We Are

High Tide by Pengelly's ("the Venue", "we", "us") operates from Looe, Cornwall. This notice explains how we collect, use and protect your personal information as a member of our team. The data controller is the proprietor of High Tide. Direct any questions to the Floor Manager.

2. What Personal Data This System Holds

This rota system holds only: your name, your role or job title, your regular days off, and any holiday or availability requests you submit. It holds no financial information, payroll data, bank details, pay rates, or sensitive personal data. Those are managed separately under your terms of engagement.

3. Why We Hold It — Legal Basis

We use your details to organise shift rotas, manage cover and communicate your schedule. Our legal basis under Article 6(1)(b) of the UK GDPR is performance of contract — this information is necessary for us to fulfil our obligations to you as your employer or engager.

4. Who Can See What

The rota — showing who works which shifts and dates — is visible to all team members. This is standard hospitality practice, equivalent to a printed rota on a noticeboard. Personal contact details, if held separately, are accessible to management only. No team member can view another's personal profile through this system.

5. How Long We Keep It

Your details remain in this system for the duration of your engagement. Within 30 days of your role ending, your personal details will be removed. Historical rota patterns may be retained for up to 12 months for operational planning.

6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

You have the right to: access the personal data we hold; correct inaccuracies; request deletion of your data (subject to legal retention obligations); and object to how we process it. To exercise any right, speak to the Floor Manager in the first instance.

7. Complaints

You may lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): www.ico.org.uk  ·  0303 123 1113.

This notice relates solely to the High Tide staff rota system. It is not a substitute for a full employment contract or comprehensive data protection policy. Please retain a copy for your records.

Data Sync

All rota data is stored in this browser only. To use the same settings on another device, export and import the data file. Keep a backup before importing.

⚠ Import overwrites all current data including rota assignments, staff, shifts, and closures. This cannot be undone.

Note: For real-time multi-device sync, this app needs a backend. See the project changelog for upgrade options.
